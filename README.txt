PUT YOUR THREE PHOTOS HERE
==========================

Exact file names:

  front.webp   -> front view (main photo, centre)
  left.webp    -> left side view
  right.webp   -> right side view

Tips
- Portrait 4:5 ratio, e.g. 1200 x 1500 px, so nothing is cropped or stretched.
- Similar lighting and background in all three.
- Keep each file under about 250 KB (compress free at https://squoosh.app).
- If your photos are .jpg or .png, either convert them to .webp, or open index.html
  in Notepad and change the three  src="images/front.webp"  (left, right) lines to .jpg / .png.

Until the files exist, labelled placeholders ("AS") are shown instead.
