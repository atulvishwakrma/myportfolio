About me

Learning by building real things

I'm Atul, from Ghazipur in Uttar Pradesh. My work sits where web development, WordPress and search come together: building pages, keeping websites healthy, and making sure the people they are meant for can actually find them.

My formal grounding comes from the BCA programme at Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University, Jaunpur. Around it I have been adding practical skills through ADCA Pro, SEO and WordPress training, and by building things. I mix hands-on coding with AI-assisted coding to prototype quickly while I keep learning the fundamentals underneath.

I also write SEO-optimised news articles in English and Hindi, so I think about websites from the reader's side of the screen as much as the developer's.

  • Web development

    Responsive, fast, readable websites. Starting with the fundamentals and growing from there.

  • WordPress & SEO

    Managing sites, researching keywords and writing content that is useful to read and easy to find.

  • English + Hindi writing

    News-style articles written for readers first and search engines second.

Where I'm heading

  • Add app development to my toolkit

How I work

From first question to launch and beyond

The seven steps I follow on a project, so nothing important gets skipped.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Understand the problem

    Who is this for and what should it help them do?

  2. 02

    Step 2: Research and planning

    Look at similar sites, list the pages and content, pick the tools.

  3. 03

    Step 3: UI/UX design

    Sketch the layout, choose type and colour, think about small screens first.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Development

    Build in small, reusable pieces and keep the code readable.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Testing and security

    Check every device size, every link and every form, and review the basics of security.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Deployment

    Publish with HTTPS, set up the domain and submit the sitemap.

  7. 07

    Step 7: Maintenance and improvement

    Fix, update and improve based on what real visitors do.
